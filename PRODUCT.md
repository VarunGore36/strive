# Product

## Register

product

## Users

STRIVE serves bank and contact-center operators who need to judge voice-call risk while handling a sensitive request. It also serves technical reviewers and hackathon judges who need to understand what the prototype proves, what evidence is available, and where the research boundaries remain.

## Product Purpose

STRIVE continuously analyzes call audio for voice-cloning and source-change risk, keeps acoustic authenticity separate from business context, and turns elevated risk into a hold-and-verify workflow. Success means an operator can understand the current evidence, see uncertainty rather than a false safe result, and protect a sensitive action without relying on the potentially compromised voice channel.

## Brand Personality

Vigilant, calibrated, protective. The interface should feel like a precision monitoring instrument: composed under pressure, technically credible, and clear about uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic hackathon dashboard, a neon cyberpunk command center, a purple-gradient AI landing template, or a decorative analytics page full of interchangeable cards. Avoid visual effects that compete with risk states, claims that imply identity verification, and motion that obscures live operational feedback.

## Design Principles

1. Put the operator's next safe action ahead of visual spectacle.
2. Make uncertainty, evidence freshness, and system boundaries visible.
3. Reserve semantic color for real system states and risk changes.
4. Use motion to show signal flow, connection, capture, and state transitions.
5. Keep the public story compelling while preserving scientific honesty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Support reduced motion, visible focus, semantic landmarks, live-region announcements, non-color state labels, responsive layouts, and clear language for users who are not machine-learning specialists.
